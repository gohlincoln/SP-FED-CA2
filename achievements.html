Industry Collaboration Banner

Singapore Polytechnic Partners with Three Regional Universities.

Singapore Polytechnic (SP) has signed MoUs with Ateneo de Manila University, Bina Nusantara University, and King Mongkut’s Institute of Technology Ladkrabang to launch the “Temasek Foundation – Singapore Polytechnic Design Thinking and Innovation in Universities Programme” at the DT|UX Summit 2022. Funded by Temasek Foundation, the three-year program will benefit up to 150 faculty members across these universities through Summit participation and workshops.
